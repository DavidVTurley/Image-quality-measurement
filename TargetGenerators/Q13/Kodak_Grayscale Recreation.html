⚑ Notes & Flags
Patch values derived from optical density: R = 10^(−D), Y = R, L* from CIE 1976.
This matches the theoretical Q13 density model. Real printed cards may deviate due to ink dot gain, paper OBA, surface finish.
L* steps are NOT equal — density spacing is logarithmic, so shadows crowd together visually.
eciRGB v2 encoding: pixel = round(L*/100 × 255). ICC profile embedded in TIFF tag 34675.
PNG export tone-maps to sRGB gamma for correct display — no ICC profile embedded.
